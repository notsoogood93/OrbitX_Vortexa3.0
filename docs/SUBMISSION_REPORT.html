1. Problem Understanding

Higher education institutions—such as Dr. D. Y. Patil Institute of Technology, Pimpri—operate as high-density academic campuses housing thousands of students, faculty, and research personnel. Every semester generates thousands of operational service requests spanning:

  • Campus Facilities & Infrastructure: Laboratory power line trips, classroom smart-board/projector failures, hostel water pump outages.
  • Academic IT & Computing Systems: GPU workstation driver freezes in Computer Labs, departmental Wi-Fi AP failures, server link drops.
  • Administrative & Equipment Requests: Classroom allocation shifts, asset transfers, and equipment calibration clearances.

Despite campus ERP portals (e.g., Samarth eGov, basic Google Forms, or standard ticketing tools), request lifecycles systematically collapse due to five structural failure modes:

1. The "Limbo" Problem

Tickets enter "In Progress" and sit for weeks without technician progression forcing or SLA accountability.

2. Premature & Ghost Closures

Technicians mark tickets "Closed" without physical repair photos, supervisor sign-off, or student confirmation.

3. Silent SLA Breaches

Deadlines expire unnoticed because legacy databases lack daemons to auto-escalate cases to HODs or the Registrar.

4. The Flaky AI Trap

Unconstrained AI chatbots hallucinate status jumps, approve invalid closures, or drop records when given mutation rights.

Critical Failure: Relational database rows in legacy ERPs are mutable. If a laboratory hazard is neglected, traditional databases cannot cryptographically prove when deadlines were missed or if historical records were retroactively modified.

2. Proposed Solution: GovFix (University Track)

GovFix is an institutional-grade, backend-enforced finite-state workflow engine purpose-built for university campus maintenance and IT operations, governed by one ironclad statutory principle:

"Deterministic Rules Govern Authority, State Transitions & Deadlines; AI Informs But Never Overrides."

GovFix replaces ad-hoc ticketing with five tightly coupled engineering subsystems:

  • Deterministic Finite State Machine (FSM): Formally defines the exact lifecycle: Draft → Submitted → Triaged → Assigned → Accepted → In Progress → Ready for Closure / Escalated → Closed or Reopened. Illegal moves (e.g. Submitted → Closed) return HTTP 409 Conflict (ERR_ILLEGAL_STATE_JUMP).
  • Backend-Enforced Role RBAC: Separates duties across Requesters, Department Supervisors, Technicians, and Registrars. Unauthorized attempts return HTTP 403 Forbidden (ERR_ROLE_UNAUTHORIZED) and are logged to audit.
  • Campus SLA & Multi-Tier Auto-Escalation Daemon: Assigns deadlines by urgency (Hazard: 12h, IT Server: 24h, Routine: 48h). Background daemons auto-escalate overdue tickets to Tier-1 (Department Supervisor) and Tier-2 (Campus Registrar).
  • Duplicate Escalation Prevention: Suppresses redundant escalation events for the same request across evaluation cycles.
  • Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Cryptographic Audit Ledger: Surpassing basic SQLite, every accepted state transition, refused unauthorized move, and escalation event is cryptographically hashed and chained into an immutable ledger with 1-click verification.
  • Virtual Time-Travel Simulation: Built-in simulation controls (+12h, +24h, +48h) allow evaluators to witness live automated escalations in seconds without altering system OS clocks.

4. Constrained AI Integration (FreeLLMAPI & Offline Heuristics)

In strict compliance with hackathon safety constraints: AI operates as a non-authoritative advisory sidecar with zero state-mutation authority.

  • Room & Asset Identification: Automatically extracts room codes (e.g., "Lab 304 - Computer Engineering") and equipment tags from request text.
  • Recurrence & Duplicate Detection: Identifies repeated failures in the same classroom or lab within 48 hours to flag chronic hardware defects.
  • Bilingual Draft Generation: Generates suggested acknowledgment templates in both English and Marathi without write privileges.
  • 100% Offline Resilience: If external APIs disconnect, embedded keyword and regex heuristics take over instantly with zero downtime.
